## Warm, clear help.

We help you understand the next step for your taxes and your personal information. We explain what is included and what may cost extra before we begin additional work. A licensed attorney reviews the work.

## One daily price

The Personal plan is {shared:personal_plan.price}, or an Abraham Lincoln, a day. It requires a $2,000 minimum retainer. It also gives you access to Notations, which start at $50. We will ask you to add funds 60 days before your daily credits run out. Work outside the plan and government fees cost extra. We will explain the cost before we begin additional work.

## Three ways we can help

Taxes: Help with federal and state tax filings. We will explain what information we need and whether additional work costs extra.

Personal information: Requests to companies that hold your personal data, asking them to remove it. We cannot promise that every company will remove every record.
