## You have a place here.

The litigation practice is plaintiff and defense. We take the case that walks in, as long as we are not conflicted out. The work we seek is the dispute that changes a person's situation, and that can change it for people like them.

We work as a team. You can meet us, and we bring in the specialists a matter needs. Speed is how we work. A person who is not heard loses ground whether or not the case is close. Moving early is how we keep that from happening quietly.

We are also company counsel, a personal legal plan, and the lawyers for a will, a formation, or a filing. That work is the rest of the firm, not a sideline. Pick the door that fits.
